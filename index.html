Tradie Track Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

Tradie Track (“the App”) helps tradespeople manage customers, jobs, quotes, invoices, payments and business expenses.

1. Information You Enter

Depending on how you use the App, you may enter business details, customer names and contact information, job details, invoice information, payment records, expense records, photographs and other business information.

2. Where Your Information Is Stored

The current version of Tradie Track is designed to store information locally on your device. Tradie Track does not operate a server for collecting this information as part of its local-storage functionality.

If you choose to send an invoice or other information using email or another service, that information may be processed by the service you choose. Its own privacy policy will apply.

3. How Information Is Used

Information entered into the App is used to provide its business-management features, including organising jobs, preparing invoices, tracking payments and calculating business totals.

4. Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with a recipient or service provider when you choose to send or export it using a feature on your device.

5. Photos, Biometrics and Device Features

If you choose to add photographs, they may be used in your job or invoice records. If you use a device authentication feature such as Face ID, authentication is handled by your device and its operating system. Tradie Track does not receive your Face ID biometric data.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you remove it or remove the App. Before uninstalling the App or changing devices, make your own backup of any business records you need to keep. Information already sent through another service may remain with that service or its recipients.

7. Security

Use a secure device passcode and keep your device software up to date. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Tradie Track is intended for people conducting or managing trade and business activities. It is not designed for children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its practices change. The latest version will be published on this webpage.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Tradie Track team using the support contact provided on the App Store listing.
