Tradie Track Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Tradie Track (“the App”) helps tradespeople manage customers, jobs, quotes, invoices, payments and business expenses.

1. Information You Enter

You may enter business details, customer names and contact details, job details, quotes, invoice information, payment and expense records, and job photographs. Please only enter information you are authorised to provide.

2. Account Information and Supabase

Tradie Track uses Supabase for account authentication. When you create or use an account, your email address and authentication-related information may be processed by Supabase to create your account, verify sign-in and maintain account security. Supabase processes this information under its own service terms and privacy practices.

3. Storage of Business Information

The current version of Tradie Track is designed to store business records locally on your device. This includes information such as customers, jobs, invoices, expenses and photographs where those features are used. These records are not automatically backed up or synchronised to our server. Uninstalling the App, losing your device or clearing its app data may cause locally stored records to be lost.

Account authentication through Supabase is separate from local business-record storage. Deleting your account does not necessarily delete business records stored locally on your device.

4. Photographs and Files

If you add job photographs or other files, they are handled by the App's relevant device features. Do not assume that photographs or files are uploaded to cloud storage or backed up unless the App explicitly provides that functionality.

5. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide the App's business-management features, authenticate accounts, maintain account security, and help users manage their own business records. We do not state that information is used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

6. Sharing and Third-Party Services

Supabase provides account-authentication services and may process information required for that purpose. If you choose to share or send information using another service, that service's own privacy policy may apply. We do not claim that local business records are transmitted to us when the App stores them only on your device.

7. Data Security

Keep your device secure and protect your account credentials. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Business records stored locally remain on your device until you delete them, remove the App or clear its data. Account information processed by Supabase is subject to the applicable service configuration and retention practices. Deleting an account may not automatically remove locally stored business records, so check the device and manage those records separately.

9. Children's Privacy

Tradie Track is a business-management app intended for tradespeople and business users, not for children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. The effective date above indicates the most recent policy date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Tradie Track app publisher through the contact details provided in the App Store listing.
