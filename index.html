# Tradie Track Privacy Policy **Effective date:** 11 October 2026 Tradie Track (“the App”) helps tradespeople manage customers, jobs, quotes, invoices, payments and business expenses. ## 1. Information You Enter Depending on how you use the App, you may enter business details, customer names and contact information, job details, invoice information, payment records, expense records, photographs and other business information. ## 2. Where Your Information Is Stored Tradie Track is currently designed to store information locally on your device. We do not operate a Tradie Track server that collects this information as part of the current local-storage functionality. Please note that information may be handled by other services when you choose to use their features, such as sending an invoice by email. Their own privacy policies apply to those services. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information entered into the App is used to provide its business-management features, including organising jobs, preparing invoices, tracking payments and calculating business totals. ## 4. Sharing Information We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with a recipient or service provider when you choose to send or export it using a feature on your device. ## 5. Photos, Biometrics and Device Features If you choose to add photographs, those photographs may be used in your job or invoice records. If you use a device authentication feature such as Face ID, authentication is handled by your device and its operating system. Tradie Track does not receive your Face ID biometric data. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Information stored locally remains on your device until you remove it or remove the App. Before uninstalling the App or changing devices, make your own backup of any business records you need to keep. Information previously sent through another service may remain with that service or its recipients. ## 7. Security Use a secure device passcode and keep your device software up to date. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 8. Children's Privacy Tradie Track is a business-management app intended for people conducting or managing trade and business activities. It is not designed for children. ## 9. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its practices change. The latest version will be published at this webpage. ## 10. Contact Us For privacy questions or requests, contact the Tradie Track team through the support contact provided on the App's App Store listing. **Important:** This policy describes the intended local-storage design. Before publishing, the developer should confirm that the released version and any included third-party services actually operate this way.
